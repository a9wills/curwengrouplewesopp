01 — Investment Summary

A freehold South London asset with multiple exit routes and a strong specialist education angle.

21-29 Sandringham Road is a substantial two-storey former bakery production and distribution facility extending to approximately 1,893.55 sq m / 20,382 sq ft GIA. The property is offered for sale freehold with vacant possession at a guide price of £2,800,000.

The investor thesis is centred on optionality: continued production, storage and distribution use; owner-occupation; alternative commercial use; SEN school repositioning; or residential / mixed-use development, all subject to the necessary planning and technical due diligence.

03 — Location

An accessible South London location close to retail, residential and transport infrastructure.

Situated on Sandringham Road, the property occupies a convenient Thornton Heath position within easy reach of the area's established retail and residential catchment. Thornton Heath town centre is close by, providing day-to-day amenities, shops, cafes and services.

The location benefits from regular rail services from Thornton Heath station into Central London and Croydon, with Selhurst station also within approximately 1 km. Nearby bus routes connect Thornton Heath with Croydon, Norbury, Streatham and the wider surrounding area.

08 — Due Diligence

Key checks before an operator-facing issue.

These items should be progressed before a binding investment case or operator commitment is relied upon.

Required before area schedule and capacity are stated.

Planning Advice

Confirm existing use, proposed school use and any change-of-use route.

Building Condition

Review structure, services, roof, drainage, asbestos, fire and accessibility.

Safeguarding

Confirm secure line, reception control, pupil movement and visitor management.

Highways

Assess drop-off, parking, minibus access, servicing and peak-hour operation.

Commissioning Case

Evidence Croydon and surrounding borough SEN demand, placement patterns and operator appetite.
