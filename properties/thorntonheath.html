01 — Maypole Concept Summary

A building strategy shaped around one-to-one teaching, low pupil mixing and calm arrival.

It is intended to show how 21-29 Sandringham Road could be planned around a specialist one-to-one SEN model where pupils have individual teaching spaces and limited mixing with other pupils.

The core design idea is simple: use the scale of the former bakery building to create a controlled, carefully managed education environment with 25-30 potential one-to-one classrooms, supporting therapy / staff spaces and a secure arrival sequence.

04 — One-to-One Classroom Model

A room strategy for pupils who need individual space and minimal peer mixing.

Maypole's model requires a different type of school environment from a conventional classroom-led setting. The building can be reviewed around 25-30 one-to-one teaching rooms, each supporting one pupil and one teacher in a calm, predictable space.

The wider floorplate allows teaching rooms to sit alongside therapy, decompression, staff, welfare and support spaces, reducing the need for pupils to mix or move through busy shared areas during the day.

06 — Secure Staggered Drop-Off

A controlled arrival sequence designed around privacy, safeguarding and reduced anxiety.

The garage / loading arrangement can be explored as a secure drop-off bay. Vehicles could enter through the opened shutter, the shutter could close behind the vehicle, and the pupil could then be received within a controlled enclosed environment.

A vehicle turntable could also be explored within the bay, allowing taxis to drive in, rotate and exit in forward gear. Once the pupil transfer is complete, the shutter reopens and the vehicle exits. This supports staggered arrivals, limits pupil mixing at the front of the building and creates a calmer handover process for students who need predictable transitions.

Step 1

Vehicle enters the shuttered bay.

Step 2

Shutter closes to create a secure transfer point.

Step 3

Pupil is received by staff away from public frontage.

Step 4

Turntable allows the taxi to exit forward before the next staggered arrival.
