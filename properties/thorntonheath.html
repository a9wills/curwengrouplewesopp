01 — Maypole Concept Summary

A building strategy shaped around one-to-one teaching, with versitile accomodation to provide a modern, comfortable and secure environment.

It is intended to show how 21-29 Sandringham Road, Thornton Heath CR7 7AX could be planned around a specialist one-to-one SEN model where pupils have individual teaching spaces and controlled mixing with other pupils.

The core design idea is simple: use the scale of the former bakery building to create a controlled, carefully managed education environment with 30 + potential 1:1 classrooms, supporting therapy / staff spaces and a secure arrival sequence.

05 — Secure Staggered Drop-Off

A controlled arrival sequence designed around privacy, safeguarding and reduced anxiety.

The existing garage can be explored as a secure drop-off bay. The secure shutters ill open upon arrival of the taxi then close behind the vehicle. The pupil could then be received within a controlled enclosed environment.

A vehicle turntable could also be explored within the bay, allowing taxis to drive in, rotate and exit in forward gear. Once the pupil transfer is complete, the shutter reopens and the vehicle exits. This supports staggered arrivals, limits pupil mixing at the front of the building and creates a calmer handover process for students who need predictable transitions.

Step 1

Vehicle enters the shuttered bay.

Step 2

Shutter closes to create a secure transfer point.

Step 3

Pupil is received by staff away from public frontage.

Step 4

Turntable allows the taxi to exit forward before the next staggered arrival.
