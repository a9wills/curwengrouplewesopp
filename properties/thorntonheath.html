03 — Location

An accessible South London location close to amenities, homes and transport infrastructure.

Situated on Sandringham Road, the property occupies a convenient Thornton Heath position within easy reach of the area's established local catchment. Thornton Heath town centre is close by, providing day-to-day amenities, shops, cafes and services.

The location benefits from regular rail services from Thornton Heath station into Central London and Croydon, with Selhurst station also within approximately 1 km. Nearby bus routes connect Thornton Heath with Croydon, Norbury, Streatham and the wider surrounding area.
