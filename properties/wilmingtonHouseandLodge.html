06 — Potential Nursery Concept & External Areas

Outdoor play, drop-off and landscaped learning environments.

The whole-site layout allows nursery functions to be distributed across the premises, supporting a clearer balance between indoor accommodation, outdoor play, parking/drop-off and operational access.

Operator concern addressed: Feedback on the House-only scheme focused on the limited open areas and the effect this could have on child numbers and day-to-day performance.

Integrated operational space: Wilmington Lodge can carry functions that would otherwise compete with nursery rooms or outdoor layout.

Better site balance: The concept allows play, vehicle circulation, arrival, support space and nursery accommodation to sit in defined zones.

Stronger nursery environment: The proposed outdoor view presents a warmer, more complete setting for operators assessing quality and commercial performance.

Subject to approvals: Capacity, layout, outdoor ratios, planning and Ofsted/operator requirements should be confirmed through detailed due diligence.
