01 - Executive Summary

A High Street nursery with a substantial secure rear garden.

27-29 High Street, Carshalton SM5 3AP, UK benefits from Existing Class E use, vacant possession, a gross internal area of approximately 3,000 sq ft / 279 sq m and approximately 5,500 sq ft of secure rear garden space.

The updated CGI material helps illustrate how the nursery could feel internally, with warm natural finishes, a secure reception, baby room, toddler room, creative messy-play space, quiet/sensory room and a garden-focused early years environment.

The building layout is presented as highly efficient for a nursery of this size, supporting strong operational use of the internal space. The large rear garden also creates future potential for additional outbuildings or nursery pods, subject to design, planning and operator requirements.

The site is positioned on Carshalton High Street, with a narrow side parking area understood to accommodate around 3 or 4 cars and Carshalton High Street Car Park immediately adjacent. Any parking tariff detail should be verified before issue.

The buyer-facing investment story is supported by agreed headline lease terms: Monkey Puzzle Day Nurseries Ltd as lessee, a new 25-year lease with no breaks, £135,000 per annum rent.

03 - Lease Terms & Investment Structure

Buyer-facing heads of terms for the nursery investment.

The proposed investment is underpinned by a new lease to Monkey Puzzle Day Nurseries Ltd, with the refurbishment contribution positioned as part of the buyer's delivery and fit-out structure.

Lessee

Monkey Puzzle Day Nurseries Ltd

Term

New 25-year lease

Within the Landlord and Tenant Act 1954, with no break clauses.

Agreed Rent

£135,000

Per annum exclusive of VAT. VAT is not applicable to this transaction.

Rent Free

12 months

From lease completion, during which time no rent is payable.

Refurbishment Allowance

£200,000

A £200,000 capital contribution is allowed for within the transaction and should be described to buyers as supporting the refurbishment / fit-out works. Payment timing and mechanism are to be agreed in the formal legal documentation between the parties' solicitors.

Rent Reviews

Reviewed at the end of each 5th year, upward only, based on compounded RPI movement between review dates, collared and capped at 1.0% and 3.0% per annum respectively.

Repairing Obligation

Full repairing and insuring lease. The lessee is to put and keep the property in good and substantial repair throughout the term.

Insurance

Payable from occupation / possession or completion of legal formalities, whichever is earlier. Insurance cost is fully payable by or recoverable from the lessee.

Business Rates

Business rates become payable from occupation / possession or completion of legal formalities, whichever is earlier.

Alterations

The lessee may carry out non-structural internal alterations without the lessor's consent, provided they do not materially adversely affect the mechanical services, overload the electrical services or affect the external appearance of the building.

All alterations must comply with statutory requirements and CDM regulations as necessary. Record drawings are to be provided to the lessor within 10 working days following completion.

Structural alterations are not permitted without the lessor's prior written consent, such consent not to be unreasonably withheld.

Conditions

  • Subject to contract and survey.
  • Vacant possession of the property being achieved.
  • Satisfactory planning permission for use of the premises as a children's day nursery, with no onerous conditions attached.
  • Satisfactory planning permission for the outside space to be used as a day nursery garden, with no onerous conditions attached.
  • Board approval by the lessee.
  • Permission for the lessee to sub-let the premises to a franchisee of their choosing.
  • Relevant conditionality in the structure of the Agreement for Lease.
